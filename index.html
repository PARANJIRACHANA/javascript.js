About ShareBite

This website helps restaurants, hotels, college hostels, supermarkets and event organizers share excess food with organizations that can distribute it to people in need.

Our Objectives

  • Reduce food wastage.
  • Connect food donors with NGOs and authorized volunteers.
  • Make food donation and collection easier.
  • Promote safe food handling and transportation.

How It Works

  1. Donor Registration: Donors register and provide their details.
  2. Food Listing: Donors add available surplus food information.
  3. Food Search: NGOs and receivers search for available donations.
  4. Food Request: Receivers request the food they need.
  5. Collection: Donors and receivers coordinate collection and delivery.

Food Safety Guidelines

  • Donate only food that is safe for consumption.
  • Maintain cleanliness during food preparation and packing.
  • Use suitable containers for storage and transportation.
  • Provide accurate preparation and safe-consumption details.
  • Arrange collection within the appropriate safe-consumption period.

Follow applicable Food Safety and Standards Authority of India (FSSAI) guidelines.
